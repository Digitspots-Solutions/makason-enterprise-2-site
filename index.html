About Us

Makason Enterprise 2 is a leading fashion and creative design business proudly serving Ikwuano and surrounding communities. We specialize in transforming fabric into wearable art, offering custom tailoring, creative fashion design, and style consulting services. Our team combines traditional Nigerian craftsmanship with contemporary design trends to create unique pieces for every occasion – from everyday wear to special events. At Amawom junction, we've built a reputation for quality, attention to detail, and delivering fashion that reflects your personal style and celebrates our rich Nigerian heritage.
